## The defect, located

`FleetTile.tsx:214-223` — `TileMetrics({ fleet })` reads `budget_used_nanos`,
`events_processed` and `updated_at` straight off the server-rendered `Fleet`
snapshot prop. The streaming hook's output at `FleetTile.tsx:109-111` feeds
`deriveTileLiveness` only. So the stream updates the liveness copy and nothing
recomputes the counters until a page load.

This is not a transport fault. `app-dev` and `api-dev` both negotiate HTTP/2,
the transport is native `EventSource` (`text/event-stream`), and that same
connection delivered the liveness transition the tile did act on.
