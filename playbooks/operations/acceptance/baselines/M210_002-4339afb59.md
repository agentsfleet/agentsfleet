## Two timeouts under machine load

The first `orly gate pr` run on `275c1e509` saw two tests time out while the
machine's load average was 18.87 (`uptime`), driven by macOS's own `suggestd`,
`syspolicyd` and `mds_stores`. The second run, on `491fe5960`, passed both.

| Test | Under the first gate run | Alone |
|---|---|---|
| `afd_outbound` `lanes::answers_to_one_destination_leave_in_the_order_they_were_queued` | timed out at its 30 s acknowledgement wait (`lanes.rs:117`) | passed in 0.49 s |
| `agentsfleetd` `integration_rust_runner::test_rust_runner_lease_roundtrip` | no lease row by its 60 s settle deadline (`integration_rust_runner.rs:129`) | passed every run below |

`afd_outbound` does not change on this branch. The round-trip test changes by
one line (the scripted memory item names its `visibility`), and the runner it
drives changes in `client.rs` and `lease_loop.rs`, so its timing was compared
against the comparison revision rather than assumed:

| Conditions | `4339afb59` | Branch |
|---|---|---|
| right after the failed gate run, datastores not reset | — | 6.79 s, 51.89 s, 5.75 s |
| freshly reset datastores, five runs each, one side after the other | 14.95, 10.86, 6.81, 7.81, 7.82 s | 9.86, 12.91, 20.82, 11.88, 10.85 s |
| six interleaved pairs, so load falls on both alike | 9.86, 9.87, 7.83, 6.81, 11.87, 7.83 s | 9.87, 12.91, 6.79, 12.91, 11.90, 6.79 s |

The interleaved pairs share a floor (6.81 s and 6.79 s) and a range, and every
time lands near a whole second, the step of the runner's 1 s no-work poll pause
(`afd_core/src/timing.rs:68`). The 51.89 s run happened only on datastores the
failed gate run had left a fleet in; `make/test-integration-rustd.mk` records
why that costs this test: a runner leases any fleet whose tags it carries, so
it can be handed fleets other scenarios left behind. The coverage lane gives it
its own datastores for that reason.
