## The Rust coverage ratchet

`RUSTD_COVERAGE_FLOOR` stays at 97. The branch measured 97.5163%, and 98%
needs 846 missed against the 1051 now standing — 205 weighted lines, roughly
147 genuinely-uncovered ones out of the 798 the workspace carries.

The floor is not raised ahead of a run that achieves it. `make/test-integration-rustd.mk:161-186`
records that exact move being made and reversed: a floor that never drops below
a number a run ACHIEVED is what keeps a regression from hiding, and 98 was set
once when the measurement was 97.0409% and returned to 97 the same day.
